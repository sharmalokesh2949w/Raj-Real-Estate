1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RAJ Real Estate ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or submit a property-related enquiry.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and transparently. This policy describes the types of information that may be collected, why it may be used, when it may be shared and the choices available to you, subject to applicable law.

By using our website or voluntarily providing information to us, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will seek it as required.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with our website and services. It may include the following categories, where applicable:

Information you provide directly

  • Name and contact details.
  • Property or project of interest.
  • Preferred location or property requirements.
  • Budget or other preferences you choose to share.
  • Messages, questions and other details you voluntarily provide.
  • Information submitted when requesting a callback or site visit.

Technical and usage information

When you access a website, certain technical information may be generated or recorded by the website or its hosting infrastructure. Depending on the systems actually in use, this may include:

  • IP address and general device or browser information.
  • Pages requested and basic website activity.
  • Date, time and technical logs relating to website requests.
  • Cookie or similar technology information, where such technologies are used.

We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for the purposes described in this policy.

3. Information You Provide

You may choose to provide personal information when you:

  • Complete a website contact form.
  • Submit a property or project enquiry.
  • Request information about a property.
  • Ask for a callback or site visit.
  • Contact us by email, phone or another available contact method.
  • Send us a message or provide additional details about your requirements.

Please provide information that is accurate and relevant to your enquiry. Avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.

4. Property Enquiry and Lead Generation

RAJ Real Estate may use information submitted through property enquiry forms to understand your requirements and respond to your interest in a property or project.

Depending on the information you provide, this may include your name, mobile number, email address, property or project of interest, preferred location, budget and message.

When you submit an enquiry, the information may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Provide information about the requested property or project.
  • Arrange a callback.
  • Schedule or coordinate a site visit.
  • Share relevant property details.
  • Assist with buying or investment enquiries.
  • Follow up regarding the enquiry you submitted.

We seek to use enquiry information for relevant property-related communication and service purposes, subject to applicable law and your choices.

5. How We Use Your Information

Subject to applicable law, we may use information collected through the website or other interactions for purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions, requests and enquiries.
  • Providing property, project or service information.
  • Communicating about a requested callback or site visit.
  • Understanding your stated property preferences.
  • Managing and maintaining enquiry records.
  • Operating, maintaining and improving the website.
  • Protecting the website and helping prevent misuse or unauthorised activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations and addressing disputes.

We will seek to use personal information in a manner relevant to the purposes for which it was collected, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

6. How We Contact You

If you submit an enquiry, we may contact you using the contact details you provide to respond to your request or discuss the property or project you asked about.

Depending on the contact methods actually offered and used by RAJ Real Estate, this may include:

  • Phone calls.
  • SMS messages.
  • Email communications.
  • WhatsApp messages or calls.

The channels listed above are possible methods of communication, not a statement that every channel is necessarily used. We will use only the channels applicable to our actual business processes and subject to applicable law.

If you receive a follow-up communication and no longer wish to receive it, you may contact us using the privacy contact details provided below. We will consider your request in accordance with applicable law.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not treat the information you submit as information that should be shared without a reason. Where necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide requested services, we may share relevant information with authorised employees, representatives, service providers, project partners or other parties involved in fulfilling your request, subject to applicable law.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, such as to respond to lawful requests or protect relevant rights and interests.

We do not intend to sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product.

The actual recipients of information depend on the services and business processes involved in handling your enquiry.

8. Service Providers and Technology Partners

Our website or business operations may rely on service providers for functions such as website hosting, technical maintenance, communications or enquiry handling, where such providers are actually engaged.

Where a service provider processes personal information on our behalf, we seek to ensure that the arrangement is appropriate for the relevant service and subject to applicable law.

We do not identify any specific technology partner in this policy unless its use has been verified for the relevant website or business process.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Cookies are small files that a website may store on your device. Similar technologies may also be used to support website functionality or understand how a website is used.

Depending on the technologies actually implemented on our website, cookies or similar technologies may be used for purposes such as:

  • Essential functionality: Supporting features required for the website to operate.
  • Performance: Helping understand technical performance and reliability.
  • Analytics: Understanding website usage, where analytics tools are implemented.
  • Preferences: Remembering certain user choices, where supported.
  • Marketing or advertising: Supporting relevant activities, where such technologies are implemented and permitted.

These categories describe possible uses of cookies and similar technologies. They are not confirmation that every category or any particular third-party analytics or advertising service is currently active on our website.

You may be able to manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

We may contact you about the property or project that you enquired about. Any additional marketing communication will be handled subject to applicable law and any consent or preferences that may be required.

Submitting a property enquiry does not mean that you must agree to unrelated marketing communications.

Where applicable, you may request that marketing communications be stopped by contacting us through the privacy contact details below.

We will consider your request in accordance with applicable law. Communications necessary to address an existing enquiry, transaction, legal obligation or other permitted purpose may be treated separately where the law allows.

11. Data Retention

We may retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable law.

The relevant period may depend on factors such as:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Providing requested services.
  • Maintaining appropriate business records.
  • Meeting legal or regulatory requirements.
  • Resolving disputes and protecting relevant rights.

We do not specify a fixed retention period here because the appropriate period may vary depending on the information and circumstances.

12. Data Security

We seek to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure.

Access to personal information should be limited to those who need it for relevant business or service purposes, where appropriate.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

You should also take care when sharing personal information online and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details through general enquiry forms.

These may include, where applicable:

  • Requesting access to personal information relating to you.
  • Requesting correction or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion or erasure where applicable.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent and withdrawal is available under applicable law.
  • Raising a concern or submitting a request about how your information is handled.
  • Requesting that certain marketing communications be stopped, where applicable.

These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements, exceptions and limitations. We may need to verify your identity or request additional information before acting on a request.
