Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. At RAJ Real Estate, we respect your personal information and aim to be transparent about how we collect, use and protect information shared through our website and property enquiry forms.

1. Introduction

RAJ Real Estate ("we", "us", "our") operates the website rajrealestate.co and offers information about residential and commercial plots and townships in Jaipur, Ajmer and Navi Mumbai. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or submit a property enquiry, how we use it, who we may share it with, and the choices available to you. By using our website or submitting your details through any form on this site, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information through the following channels:

  • Website enquiry and contact forms
  • Property/project-specific enquiry forms
  • Phone calls made to our listed numbers
  • WhatsApp conversations initiated through the website's WhatsApp button
  • Email communications
  • Site visit requests
  • Basic technical/browser information collected automatically while you browse the website

3. Information You Provide

When you fill out a form on our website (for example, a "Request Callback" or "Quick Inquiry" form), we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Mobile/contact number
  • Email address
  • The project or property you are interested in
  • Location preference and budget, where provided
  • Any message or additional information you choose to share with us

4. Property Enquiry and Lead Generation

When you submit a property enquiry, we use the information primarily to respond to your request and help you with the property or project you are interested in. This may include using your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Share details about the requested property or project
  • Arrange a callback
  • Schedule a site visit
  • Assist with buying or investment-related queries
  • Follow up regarding the enquiry you submitted

We do not intend to sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product.

5. How We Use Your Information

In addition to responding to enquiries, we may use the information we collect to maintain internal enquiry records, improve our website and services, understand which projects visitors are interested in, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

6. How We Contact You

Where you provide your contact details, we may reach out to you by phone call, SMS, email or WhatsApp to respond to your enquiry, share requested property information, or follow up on a site visit or callback request.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Where necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide requested services, we may share relevant information with authorised employees, representatives, service providers, project partners or other parties involved in fulfilling your request, subject to applicable law. We may also disclose information where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order or governmental request.

8. Service Providers and Technology Partners

Our website uses Meta Pixel, a technology that helps us understand website visits and measure the effectiveness of our online presence. We have not identified any other third-party analytics, CRM or lead-management tools integrated into this website at this time. If additional service providers are engaged in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support core website functionality, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our website (including through the Meta Pixel referenced above). Cookies generally fall into categories such as:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function correctly
  • Performance cookies — help us understand how the site is used
  • Preference cookies — remember choices you make on the site
  • Marketing/advertising cookies — used by tools such as Meta Pixel to measure engagement with our content

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the website may not function as intended if cookies are disabled.

10. Marketing Communications

Submitting an enquiry through our website may result in follow-up communication through phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp regarding the property or project you enquired about. If you no longer wish to receive such communications, you may request that we stop contacting you by reaching out through the contact details listed below, subject to applicable law.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, provide the services you have requested, meet our business and legal record-keeping requirements, and resolve any disputes that may arise.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information you share with us.

13. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have rights relating to your personal data, including the right to request access to the information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion or erasure of your information where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and raise concerns about how your information is processed.

To exercise any of these choices, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below, subject to applicable law.

14. Third-Party Websites and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or social media pages (such as Facebook, Instagram or X). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

15. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults seeking information about real estate investments. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

This Privacy Policy is provided for general informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. It is subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and may be updated as our practices or legal requirements evolve.
